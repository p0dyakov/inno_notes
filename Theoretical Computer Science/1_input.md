## Slide 4
**Text:**
**Properties**

*   These ratios converge to a constant limit which is called the **golden ratio** (also called the golden proportion, or golden section). The golden ratio is an irrational number which is defined as:
*   $\psi$ = 1.61803398…
*   One can observe that **the odd terms of the Fibonacci ratio** (the 1st, 3rd, 5th… terms) **are all less than the golden ratio**, while **the even terms of the Fibonacci ratio** (the 2nd, 4th, 6th … terms) **are all above the golden ration** [sic]
