**Part I: Find the Sum of the Following Convergent Series**

**Examples**

1.  Find the sum of the series:
    $$ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n(n+3)} $$
2.  Find the sum of the series:
    $$ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n}{1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdots (2n+1)} $$
3.  Find the sum of the series:
    $$ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\sin(\frac{1}{n(n+1)})}{\cos(\frac{1}{n})\cos(\frac{1}{n+1})} $$

**Homework Problems**

**H1.** Determine whether the series converges and find its sum if possible:
$$ \sum_{n=2}^{\infty} (\sqrt{n+2} - 2\sqrt{n+1} + \sqrt{n}) $$

**H2.** Determine whether the series converges and find its sum if possible:
$$ \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{n^2+3}{n!} $$

**Part II: Study the Convergence or Divergence of the Following Series**

**Examples**

1.  Study the convergence of:
    $$ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left(\frac{n+2}{n+3}\right)^n $$
2.  Study the convergence of:
    $$ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n^2}{2n^2+1} $$
3.  Study the convergence of:
    $$ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{\sin^4 n} $$

**Homework Problems**

**H1.** Study the convergence of:
$$ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n \sin\left(\frac{1}{n}\right) $$

**H2.** Study the convergence of:
$$ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}-\sqrt{n+1}} $$

**Part III: Study Convergence Using Comparison Tests**

**Examples**

1.  Study the convergence of:
    $$ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{\ln(e^n - 1)} $$
2.  Study the convergence of:
    $$ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{2n-1}{(\sqrt{2})^n} $$
3.  Study the convergence of:
    $$ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{2n+1}{(n+1)^2(n+2)^2} $$
4.  Study the convergence of:
    $$ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\sqrt{n}}{(n+1)\sqrt{n}} $$
